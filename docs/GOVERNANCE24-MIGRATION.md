# 24-hour business governance migration

This is source and local validation only. It does not schedule, sign, broadcast, deploy, transfer tokens, or activate mainnet changes.

`PoolTimelock24` has a hard 24-hour scheduling floor, the existing approved proposer/canceller, open execution, and self administration. Updating its configured delay below 24 hours cannot remove the floor. The original `PoolTimelock` and `PoolBeacon` source and runtime templates remain unchanged.

Each immutable legacy BeaconProxy retains its original beacon address. The two old beacons install factory-bound `Governance24Dispatcher` implementations after the original 48-hour delay. Each dispatcher has no mutable storage and forwards to a separate `Governance24Beacon`, owned permanently by the new lock. The second delegatecall preserves the original pool address, caller, value, storage, balances, and allowances. A private immutable `SELF` rejects direct fallback calls to the dispatcher; immutable getters remain readable. Both existing pools and newly created pools follow this route.

The old beacon owners and old timelock remain a **48-hour recovery route**. Those two original beacons, their ownership, and their delay are not migrated or weakened. Normal pool business logic upgrades use the secondary 24-hour beacons. This migration does not make every external chain contract or the legacy recovery route governable in 24 hours.

The only migration entry point on all four UUPS proxies is `migrateGovernance24(address expectedPreviousTimelock,address nextTimelock)`, called by the old lock in the same `upgradeToAndCall` transaction. It requires proxy context, exact stored previous lock, a different next lock, an original 48-hour hard floor, a new 24-hour hard floor and required roles, and continuity of the approved proposer. The two factories also transfer ownership to the new lock. Factory operator, treasury, lens, legacy beacon, share market, machine reservations, designated subscribers and pool histories are untouched. Each market changes only its stored timelock. No new proxy storage field is introduced.

`Governance24Validation.requireMigration` is an external **view** linked-library entry point. It reads lock properties and roles and rejects invalid migrations. It has no mutable state, state-write opcode, token transfer or arbitrary forwarding entry point. Linking keeps the fresh factory below EIP-170 without changing existing business code. The other original linked libraries remain in place; notably FirstoSale retains its original PoolFunds settlement dependency.

The atomic original-lock batch has exactly seven zero-value actions: upgrade the two legacy beacons to dispatchers, upgrade and migrate the core factory, portfolio factory and two markets, and transfer PlatformAuthority ownership. A failure in the last action rolls back every earlier implementation, owner and timelock change. The new lock governs both factory owner operations, four UUPS upgrade authorities, both business implementation beacons and Authority configuration. Signed administrator permissions, nonces and gas wallet are preserved by the migration.

The selected core PoolVault/FlexiblePurchase includes the existing single-leaf Firsto BatchAsk kind-1 path. The selected BudgetPortfolioVault includes the audited final-sale rounding fix from `6150ad8869a1a9689cc60f0832f2f1e07b0f3bb1`: settling the final child credits remaining integer wei to treasury and clears `saleRemainderWei`. It changes no layout. This only applies to projects whose final child settlement runs after activation; **already Closed legacy projects are not retroactively credited**. Any historical closed-project residue needs a separately reviewed recovery proposal.

Local integration tests build the full core/portfolio graph and create live NFT custody, BNB refunds and market claims, BEM entitlements, shares, share locks, machine reservations and open orders. They compare those business states across the atomic migration, then exercise claims, existing listings, new pool initialization, both dispatcher contexts, every subsequent UUPS/business-beacon/Authority governance route, invalid migrations, the 24-hour hard floor, and the original 48-hour recovery route. Storage validation compares full linear and ERC-7201 layouts against current bases and delivered deployment baselines.
